# AIYA Events Toolkits 🛠️

This repository provides supporting resources for the **AI Youth Alliance (AIYA)** global events program.

It contains structured exercises, challenges, technical resources, project guidelines, and framework starter kits supporting AIYA's experiential events and student participation.

Current AIYA events include:

- **AIYA Literacy Week** — hands-on challenges and exercises designed to help students develop foundational AI literacy and practical AI fluency while exploring the capabilities and limitations of artificial intelligence.
- **AIYA Global AI Hackathon** — an international student competition focused on building applications, models, agents, and solutions using artificial intelligence.
- **AIYA Impact Symposium** — a global showcase for student projects applying artificial intelligence to real-world problems and endeavors.

For current event information, participation details, dates, and announcements, visit:

**[AIYA Events](https://www.aiyouthalliance.org/events)**

## ⚠️ Academic Notice & Cognitive Well-Being

AIYA encourages students to engage with artificial intelligence critically and deliberately.

AI should be used as a **force multiplier for the mind**, not as a replacement for independent thought, analysis, subject mastery, creativity, or genuine authorship.

The resources in this repository are intended to help students explore both the capabilities and limitations of AI through direct experience.

Students should:

- Question AI-generated outputs
- Verify information independently
- Understand the limitations of AI systems
- Maintain independent analytical capacity
- Develop genuine subject understanding
- Preserve agency and authorship in their work

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone this repository to your local machine.
2. Review the toolkit corresponding to the AIYA event in which you are participating.
3. Follow the event-specific instructions, requirements, and guidelines.
4. Coordinate with your school's **Faculty or Administrative Advisor** where appropriate.
5. Visit the official **[AIYA Events](https://www.aiyouthalliance.org/events)** page for current event information and participation details.
6. For additional support, contact AIYA at **info@aiyouthalliance.org**.
